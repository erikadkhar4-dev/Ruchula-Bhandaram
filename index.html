Our Story

Honest vegetarian food, made to feel like a feast.

The name says it all. Ruchula means flavours and Bhandaram means a treasury, and that is how we cook: a full store of regional tastes under one roof.

From generous North and South Indian thalis to Indo-Chinese starters, biryani and inventive plates like bao and crisp spring rolls, every dish is strictly vegetarian and served with warm, polite hospitality.
